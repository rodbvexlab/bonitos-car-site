## Indexed architecture

Primary intended URLs:

https://www.bonitoscar.com.br/
https://www.bonitoscar.com.br/leves
https://www.bonitoscar.com.br/pesados
https://www.bonitoscar.com.br/orcamento

Google has also historically discovered variants and legacy URLs.

Before route cleanup, inspect:

- .html variants
- HTTP versus HTTPS
- www versus non-www
- legacy /servicos/
- redirects
- canonical tags
- sitemap
- 404 URLs

## Strategy

Do not pursue index count as a vanity metric.

Focus on:

- preserving ranking URLs;
- improving CTR;
- improving local intent relevance;
- increasing qualified leads;
- measuring conversions.
